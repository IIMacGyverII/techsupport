**WIRELESS PIR MOTION DETECTOR 433 MHz**
**Installation Instructions**
RF-PIR-433-D-NN

• Position the sensor to protect an area where an intruder
would be most likely to walk across the detection pattern.

• Mount the motion sensor on a rigid surface which is free
from vibrations.

• Mount the sensor permanently on a flat wall or in a corner.
Do not set it on a shelf.

• Mount the motion sensor on an insulated, outside wall
facing in.

• Position the sensor so it faces a solid reference point, like a
wall. If possible, locate sensors within 100 feet (30.5 m) of
the panel. While a transmitter may have a range of 500 feet
(152 m) or more out in the open, the environment at the
installation site can have a significant effect on transmitter
range. Sometimes a change in sensor location can help
overcome adverse wireless conditions.

**Introduction**
• Do not mount the sensor near duct work or other large
metallic surfaces which may affect the RF signals (see “Final
Testing” on page 2). Actual acceptable transmitter range
should be verified for each installation.
A motion sensor (passive-infrared or PIR) detects movement
within a specific area by sensing the infrared energy emitted
from a body as it moves across the sensor’s field of view.
When this motion is detected, the sensor transmits an alarm
signal to the control panel.

**Mounting Location**
A. Wall mounting:
Use a motion sensor to protect locations where door/window
sensors are impractical or not needed. For example, use a
motion sensor to protect large areas or open floor plans.
Motion sensors also provide backup protection for
door/window sensors.
1. Sensor base fastened flat on vertical wall (± 2
degrees).

2. Height = 7.5’-9.8’ (2.3m or 3m) above floor of
monitored area.

3. Clear line-of-sight from sensor to monitored area
The sensor features a 3 minute lockout of alarm signals.
When an alarm occurs, no additional alarm signals are sent
for the next 3 minutes. This is to increase battery life in high
traffic areas.
Note: glass will block sensor’s view.
4. Sensor aimed away from windows and reflected
sunlight.

The following installation guidelines must be met to provide
false alarm immunity.
5. Sensor aimed away from heaters or heater/cooler
outlets.

6. Sensor aimed so that likely intruder paths cross three
views.
• The sensor must be mounted on a wall surface or incline
mounted in a corner at a mounting height of 7.5 feet (2.3 m)

• The sensitivity switch must be set to Low.
• Room temperature must be kept between 60º and 120° F
(16º and 49º C).

• Do not aim the sensor at windows, fireplaces, air
conditioners, area heaters, forced air heating vents, or place
it in direct sunlight.

• Windows should be closed in any area which has an armed
motion sensor.

**Replacing the Batteries**
Battery life depends on how often the detector transmits
signals, but is more dependent on the temperature of the
installation environment. When the battery voltage gets low,
the detector transmits a low battery signal to the panel. The
yellow LED will flash once every 10 seconds to denote low
battery. The panel then activates trouble beeps to notify the
customer that the detector battery must be replaced.
Pressing the status button identifies the sensor with the low
battery.
**Battery Installation:**
Place (2) CR123 batteries in the battery holder. Be sure that
the (+) is in the correct position.

**Sensitivity setting:**
With low sensitivity, detection occurs in 3 to 5 steps. With
high sensitivity, detection occurs in 2 to 4 steps.

Note: Sensitivity jumper (J1) is only checked on
power-up. When changing sensitivity: change jumper,
remove then re-install batteries.
Replace the battery immediately when this condition occurs,
using the following battery: **(2) Panasonic CR123A 3V**
