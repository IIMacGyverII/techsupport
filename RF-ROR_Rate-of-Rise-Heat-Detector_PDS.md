Specifications

**Rate of Rise Rating:**
12° to 15ºF per minute (6.7° to 8.3C)

**Operating Temperature:**
135°F (57°C): 32 - 100°F (0 - 37°C)

194°F (90°C): 32 - 150°F (0 - 66°C)

**UL Max. Ambient Ceiling:**
100°F/150°F (37.8ºC / 65.6ºC)

**Storage Temperature:**
-30 to 167°F (-34 to 75°C)

**Relative Humidity:**
0 to 95% noncondensing

**Maximum UL Spacing:**
50ft (15.2m) x 50ft (15.2m)

**Frequencies:**
319.5 MHz (crystal-controlled) or 345 MHz (crystal-controlled)

10 years

**Stand-By Current:**
Less than 0.9 µA

62-68 minutes
