**Wireless Glass Break Detector**
RF-ARGB-319-NN

The Wireless Glass Break Detector is used to detect breaking glass that may result in entry -
sending an alarm to the control panel.

The Glass Break Detector sends signals for;
● Alarm
● Tamper
● Low Battery
Red LED light is used to convey messages on detector status. Powered is supplied by two long-life
replaceable 3-VDC, Lithium batteries.
