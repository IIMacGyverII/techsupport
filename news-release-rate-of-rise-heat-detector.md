## Page 1

For Immediate Release:
**Wireless Rate-Of-Rise Heat Detector Released - Small Size; Big Performance.**

Minneapolis, MN, July 10, 2018 – MaxOut™ Technology is pleased to release a

new line of patented wireless, Rate-Of-Rise Heat Detectors that sense a rise in

temperature - indicating a possible fire. These wireless life safety sensors, as

well as the hard-wired version, may be new but the engineers who designed and

perfected these detectors have been producing wireless security products for

over 30 years with a combined total of 200+ years of security industry

experience.

The extremely small RF-ROR series (Radio Frequency Rate-of-Rise) and the

Rate-of-Rise hardwire detectors use electronic processing to detect and report

rising temperature. Electronic processing enables the device to detect multiple

occurrences over many years; an advantage over one-shot fuse link type devices.

The micro-processor trips the on-board transmitter when the temperature at the

detector reaches the selected fixed temperature of 135°F (57°C) or 200°F (93°C)

and automatically resets after the detector cools.

“Compared to other heat detectors, the extremely small foot-print of this sensor

(2.25” D x1.5” H) makes it the small-size market leader with all the performance

and reliability of much larger sensors”, said Dennis Dop, Vice President Sales of

MaxOut Technology. “Our goal in engineering life safety and security sensors is

maximum RF range, effective performance, small size, and very small unobtrusive

footprint. Usually it is possible to achieve many of these features, but not all four.

With this new line of wireless and hardwired Rate-of-Rise detectors, we were able

to hit the mark on all of the product design goals”, adds Dop.
